OUR STORY

Designed between structure and spontaneity, KIVO creates pieces that move effortlessly between everyday life and special occasions.

The Studio Origins

Rooted in Craft, Defined by Contrast

Founded with a passion for tactile textures and quiet elegance, KIVO was born out of a desire to break away from mass-produced monotony. We approach garment and accessory creation as an ongoing dialogue between architectural silhouettes and soft, organic draping.

Every collection is thoughtfully curated to balance refined tailoring with an effortless sense of ease—giving you wardrobes that feel both intentional and entirely lived-in.

Core Ethics

Our Philosophy

Intentional Design

We reject fleeting micro-trends in favor of enduring silhouettes. Each seam, fold, and finish is carefully considered to ensure long-lasting style.

Sensory Materials

Textile selection is at the heart of what we do. From breathable linens and structured wools to soft silks, our fabrics speak through touch.

Conscious Creation

Sustainability isn’t an afterthought—it’s built into our small-batch production model, minimizing waste and honoring ethical studio standards.

Materials & Sustainability

Crafted with Respect for Tomorrow

We believe true luxury accounts for its footprint. That is why we partner with independent mills and artisans who prioritize responsible dye processes, organic fibers, and fair working environments.

By producing in intentional small batches, we ensure that surplus is minimized and every single piece finds a cherished home in your capsule wardrobe.
